40-Point Formative Rubric Standard

1. Task Achievement / Response (10 Marks):

Complete coverage of trends, key data points, overviews, and word count adherence (150w / 250w).

2. Coherence and Cohesion (10 Marks):

Clear paragraph structure, logical flow of ideas, and precise use of linking devices.

3. Lexical Resource (10 Marks):

Varied academic vocabulary, correct trend collocations, and spelling accuracy.

4. Grammatical Range and Accuracy (10 Marks):

Control of clause boundaries (no run-ons/fragments), correct subject-verb agreement, and trend prepositions.

Standardized Diagnostic Error Taxonomy

SYNTAX_RUN_ON: Two clauses joined without punctuation or conjunction.
SYNTAX_FRAGMENT: Incomplete thought or dependent clause standing alone.
TREND_PREPOSITION: Confusing "by" vs "to", "between...to", etc.
SUBJECT_VERB_AGREEMENT: Mismatch (e.g. "it were", "they was").
TENSE_INCONSISTENCY: Unjustified shift between past and present.
LEXICAL_COLLOCATION: Unnatural word pairing or incorrect register.
